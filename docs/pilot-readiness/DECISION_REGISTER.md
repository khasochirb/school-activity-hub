## Decision hygiene

- A decision is not final until `Final approval` contains the named approver, date, decision, conditions, evidence location, and next review date.
- Decisions that alter permanent product boundaries require a new governance, privacy, security, and safeguarding review.
- If D-10 or D-12 is unresolved, the pilot must remain operational-only and no research dataset may be created.
- If D-06, D-07, D-11, D-13, D-14, D-15, D-16, D-18, D-23, or D-24 is unresolved at launch review, the default outcome is **No-Go**.
