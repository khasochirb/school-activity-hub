Activity Hub, clean

Luma's structure (white hairline cards, square covers, the events timeline and a soft glow) in Activity Hub's own colours: school-blue buttons with softer square corners, a warm background, a blue and yellow glow, and calendar tiles on the timeline. Richer screens and far fewer buttons than the live site: one main action per screen, the rest in a ⋯ menu. Cover art, colour-coded categories, a week strip, who's going, goals, charts and live activity give each screen more to see at a glance. All 31 screens of the site are here. Click any screen to try it; the switch in the corner changes between student, teacher, school admin and platform admin. Everything saves in your browser only.

The rules

One main action per screen

Every screen has one blue button for the thing most people came to do. Everything else is quieter.

Secondary actions in a ⋯ menu

Share, copy link, edit, archive and print move into one menu instead of a row of buttons.

Filters are chips

Five dropdowns and tabs become one row of chips and a search box.

Details when you ask

Practical details, safety and members sit behind tabs instead of a long stack of sections.

One search for everything

Press / anywhere to find an event, club or student.

Fewer menu items

4 tabs at the top (and on phones). Everything else is under your avatar.
